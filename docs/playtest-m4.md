# Human playtest — M4

Status: script prepared; no human session recorded in this increment.

## Objective and criteria

Check whether players understand results before the next decision, notice a new turn even when the same player opens again, and can play without audio or motion. Assess clarity and fun in 1v1/2v2 and both presets. The 10–20 minute target remains a hypothesis; simulations do not measure human duration.

Before each session, record the local version, format, preset, devices, orientation and familiarity with Truco. Use fictional nicknames; do not record private cards, tokens, seeds, recordings or personal data. Local aggregate notes suffice; there is no external analytics. Sharing and recording require a separate scope.

## Script

1. Create and join by invitation, choose teams, mark ready and start without facilitator explanation.
2. Observe dealing and pass/activate preparation. Ask whether it was clear when actions became available.
3. Complete a trick. Ask players to explain who won, why and who plays next, including when the same person opens again.
4. Observe a hand ending through tricks and another through folding. Check understanding of rewards, score and the next deal. Ties/manilhas/eleven depend on natural occurrence; record unobserved cases without manipulating real cards.
5. Use all four powers when assigned; note confusing selection, invalid targets and powers perceived as useless. Confirm the distinction between face-down play and Máscara.
6. Disable audio, adjust volume, reload and resume. Compare understanding without sound. Enable reduced motion on the device and repeat part of the flow, retaining text and reading time.
7. Play with keyboard and touch, including portrait/landscape. Look for invisible focus, inaccessible controls, collisions, clipping and hard-to-read text.
8. Finish a match and request a rematch. Discuss bluffing, cooperation, frustration, pacing and desire to repeat.

## Session record

| Measure | Aggregate record |
|---|---|
| Completed matches and duration | Actual minutes by format/preset |
| Understanding of results and next opener | Unassisted / assisted / not understood |
| Pacing of 0.9 / 1.8 / 2.4 s pauses | Short / appropriate / long, with reason |
| Audio and effects | Useful / distracting / tiring; device and volume |
| Assigned and used powers | Counts without revealing cards or seeds |
| Accessibility and layout issues | Reproducible public steps and impact |
| Fun and desire for a rematch | Aggregate observations without names |

Record limitations and unexercised scenarios. Changes to costs, rules or decision times require approval; do not adjust automatically or add powers to compensate for low usage. Presentation intervals are reversible defaults documented in [ADR 008](adr/008-authoritative-presentation-pauses.md).
